What we do

Clear about the limits

  • Sort your bills, invoices and receipts into a live spreadsheet

  • Send you a weekly PDF summary
  • Flag blurry receipts and odd charges instead of guessing

Questions

Common questions

Will you contact my vendors or customers?

No. Clear Desk works only with you. Every reminder and summary comes to you, and you decide what to pay and when.

How do you know when I have paid a bill?

Tick it off in your spreadsheet, or reply to a reminder with the bill number, like "paid 3." If a bill looks late, we check with you first, since you may have paid it another way.

Do you need access to my bank account?

No. We never ask for bank logins or access to payment systems. We only work with the bills and receipts you choose to send, and you pay your bills yourself.

What does it cost after the free week?

We agree on a simple monthly price with you before the free week ends. You are never charged without your yes.

Who sees my documents?

Only Richard, and only what you choose to share. We use them to do the work you asked for, and we delete your files if you ask.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Either side can end the pilot at any time, and you keep your spreadsheet either way.
